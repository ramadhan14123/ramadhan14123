<p align="center"><i>a record kept by a wandering knight, since 2021</i></p>

<br>

It started in 2021. No map was given — so one had to be drawn along the way, step by step, through terrain no one else marked out first. No mentor walked beside; hard work and the refusal to quit became the only teacher that stayed. There were nights spent staring at a broken build like a dragon that refused to die, and small victories that felt bigger than they should've. Nobody hands you armor at the start. You forge it yourself, one failed attempt at a time, until it finally fits.

**Two blades ride along on this road:** *The Serpent's Fang* — quiet, patient, strikes clean. And *The Wandering Blade* — restless, shifting, never sits still. <i>The blades may change with the road, but the craft remains.</i>

Somewhere further down the path, something new has been stirring — a presence that doesn't fit the old maps.

> It may become a powerful companion. It may become a weapon worth wielding. Or perhaps... it may be the monster that comes for the knight.

The road keeps unfolding. What's written here is only what's been walked so far.
